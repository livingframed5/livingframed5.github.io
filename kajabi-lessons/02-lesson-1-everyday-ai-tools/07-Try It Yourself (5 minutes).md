## Try It Yourself (5 minutes)

1. Open your chosen AI tool.
2. Write ONE email your business actually needs this week — a bid cover, a delay notice, or a
   follow-up — using the 5-part prompt.
3. Read the draft. Change anything that's wrong or that "doesn't sound like you."
4. Save your 5-part prompt somewhere (Notes app is fine) — you'll reuse it.
