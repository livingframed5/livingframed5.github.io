## Part 4 — Marketing & Social Posts Without the Blank Page

Concrete work is local. AI is great at turning one good job photo + a few facts into a post.

> "You are a social media writer for a concrete contractor. Write a Facebook post about this job:
> stamped concrete patio, 1,100 sq ft, new homeowner, finished last week. Include the contractor's
> name (Lakeview Concrete). Tone: friendly, local, proud of the work. End with 'Call for a free
> quote' and a phone number placeholder. Keep it under 120 words."

Repeat for Google Business, before/after posts, and even a short ad. Feed it the same facts,
ask for a different format. One job = a whole month of posts.

> **Pro tip:** always review the post. AI doesn't know the job's backstory (the tricky access,
> the days of rain, the client who nearly cancelled). You add the truth, it adds the speed.
