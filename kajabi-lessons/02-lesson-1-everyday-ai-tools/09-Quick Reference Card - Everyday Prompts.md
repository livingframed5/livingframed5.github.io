## Quick Reference Card — Everyday Prompts

```
EMAIL →  ROLE: bid writer/office manager. TASK: draft email. CONTEXT: facts + tone. FORMAT: short. CHECK: missing details.
SUMMARY → "Summarize in bullets: what's asked, deadlines, costs, my action items. Mark MISSING for missing numbers."
SITE NOTES → "Turn my notes into a dated job log: What happened / Crew / Materials / Issues / Next steps."
SOCIAL → "Write a Facebook post about [job]. Tone: local and proud. End with a call-to-action."
VOICE → "Clean up this voice note into a short professional email: [transcript]."
```
