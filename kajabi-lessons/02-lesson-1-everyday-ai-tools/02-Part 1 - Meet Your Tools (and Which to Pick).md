## Part 1 — Meet Your Tools (and Which to Pick)

You don't need to be a tech person. All you need is a free account and a browser or app.

| Tool | Best for | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **ChatGPT** (chatgpt.com) | All-around drafting, reasoning, file uploads | Free tier is plenty |
| **Claude** (claude.ai) | Long documents, clear writing, careful answers | Free tier is plenty |
| **Google Gemini** (gemini.google.com) | Ties into Gmail/Google Docs, search smarts | Free tier is plenty |
| **Microsoft Copilot** (in Word/Outlook) | Already in your Microsoft apps | Free/Included |

**My recommendation for a contractor:** pick **one** chat app and get good at it. Any of the three
big ones works. Switching apps later is easy because the skill transfers — the prompts are the same.

> **Tool note:** tools update constantly and screenshots age fast. Don't chase versions — chase
> the *method*. Every tool here can do the tasks in this lesson today.
