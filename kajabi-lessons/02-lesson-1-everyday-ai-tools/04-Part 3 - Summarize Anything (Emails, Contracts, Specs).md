## Part 3 — Summarize Anything (Emails, Contracts, Specs)

AI's single best everyday trick: **paste it in, get the short version.** This saves you the
20-minute read on the 6-email chain from the GC or the 30-page spec sheet.

> "Summarize this in 4 bullet points: what's being asked, any deadlines, any costs, and anything
> that needs my action. If a number is missing, write 'MISSING' in its place. Use plain English."

**Rules of thumb:**
- Paste emails, RFPs, spec excerpts, scope letters, lease/contract pages — anything text-based.
- For PDFs/Word docs, upload the file to ChatGPT or Claude and ask the same thing.
- Always ask it to flag **action items** and **dates** — that's where you get your day back.
- Don't paste a whole contract to a free tool. Redact names/addresses first (see Lesson 5).
