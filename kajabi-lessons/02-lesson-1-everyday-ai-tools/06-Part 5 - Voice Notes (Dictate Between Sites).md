## Part 5 — Voice Notes (Dictate Between Sites)

Use your phone's AI app's voice feature to dictate while you're driving (hands-free) or walking a
site, then have AI clean it up.

> "Turn this voice note into a tidy email to the GC: [dictated text]. Keep my meaning, fix the
> grammar, keep it short."

Try it: speak a rambling 60-second update about a job, then paste the transcript in and watch it
become a clean paragraph. This one habit saves most contractors 2–3 hours a week.
