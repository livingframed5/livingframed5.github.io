## Quick Reference Card — Site Prompts

```
DAILY LOG   → "Turn this update into a job log: Job / Date / Crew / Work / Materials / Issues / Next steps."
POUR PLAN   → "Draft a day-by-day plan. Mark every assumption 'CONFIRM'. I'll fix the facts."
PHOTO QA    → "Describe this photo factually for QA. Say CAN'T VERIFY for anything unclear. Don't guess."
PHOTO SAFETY→ "List only clearly visible safety hazards. Say so if nothing is clear."
PUNCH LIST  → "Group these into a numbered checklist; flag the 3 most likely to delay final."
MORNING     → "Here's my day + forecast: [paste]. List weather conflicts, due items, top 2 to not slip."
```
