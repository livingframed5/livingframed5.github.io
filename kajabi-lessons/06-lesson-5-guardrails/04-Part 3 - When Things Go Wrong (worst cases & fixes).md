## Part 3 — When Things Go Wrong (worst cases & fixes)

**The AI gave me a wrong quantity and I used it.** Fix it *before* the client sees it — run the
verification. If it already went out: correct it fast and honestly, same as you'd fix any
estimate error. Own it; it happens to everyone. The rule is you check before you send.

**The AI "hallucinated" a code, product, or spec.** This is common with anything niche. Never
trust AI for: local building codes, chemical/mix data, SDS/MSDS, safety regs, or warranty rules.
**Always verify against the actual source** (the code book, the SDS, the manufacturer's sheet).

**A client asks for AI-generated content.** No rule against using AI in your bid — but don't let
a client think a human personally analyzed their job if that's not true. Be honest about how you
estimated. Transparency builds trust.

**A client sends you data (drawings, their pricing, their plans).** Treat it as confidential.
Don't paste their proprietary documents into free tools either — same rule as your own.
