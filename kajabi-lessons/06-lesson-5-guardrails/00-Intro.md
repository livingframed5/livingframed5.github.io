# Lesson 5 — AI Guardrails, Privacy & Safety

**Time:** ~30 minutes
**Tools:** none — this lesson is about judgment and habits.

> **Why this lesson exists:** AI tools make mistakes, and they remember everything you give them.
> For a concrete contractor, the two real risks are (1) an AI draft that sounds right but has the
> wrong numbers in a bid you sign, and (2) client/employee/cost data you didn't mean to share.
> This lesson is short, but it's the one that keeps you safe. Read it before you paste anything
> sensitive from Lessons 1–4.
