## Part 1 — What AI Is, in 30 Seconds

AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude, and Google Gemini are **autocomplete engines on steroids**. They've
read a huge amount of text and learned to predict what a good response looks like. When you give
them a clear question or task, they generate a useful draft back.

That's it. They're not "thinking." They're producing the most-likely-useful answer. The practical
implication for you:

- **They're fast** — a draft bid email in 15 seconds, not 20 minutes.
- **They're helpful at starting** — the hardest part of any document is the blank page.
- **They're wrong sometimes** — confident-sounding mistakes. That's why Lesson 5 exists.
