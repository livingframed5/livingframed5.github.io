## Try It Yourself (2 minutes, optional)

Open any free AI tool (ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini). Paste this:

> "You are a friendly explainer. Explain to a concrete contractor in 5 sentences why an AI draft
> can be wrong even when it sounds confident. Use one example about concrete quantities."

See how it answers. Notice the tone: confident. That's exactly why you'll always CHECK.
