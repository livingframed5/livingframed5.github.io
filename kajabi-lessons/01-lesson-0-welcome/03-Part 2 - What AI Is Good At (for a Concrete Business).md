## Part 2 — What AI Is Good At (for a Concrete Business)

| AI is genuinely good at | AI is NOT good at |
|---|---|
| Drafting text: bids, emails, marketing, job descriptions | Making final decisions — you're still the boss |
| Summarizing: meeting notes, emails, specs, long docs | Getting real numbers right without you checking |
| First-draft estimates and rough quantities from YOUR inputs | Estimating from a photo without calibration |
| Explaining things in plain English (contracts, codes) | Anything involving current, exact, local facts (prices, permits, weather) unless you give it the facts |
| Brainstorming and options | Judging a human being (client, employee) |
| Converting notes → clean documents | Working with no information — garbage in, garbage out |
| Standardizing: same format every time | Replacing your experience and your license |

The golden rule of this entire module:

> **AI drafts, you decide.**
