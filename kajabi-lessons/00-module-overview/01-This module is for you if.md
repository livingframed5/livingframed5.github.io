## This module is for you if...

You run a concrete business — flatwork, driveways, foundations, stamped, exposed aggregate,
or ready-mix — and the office side of things is eating your day:

- Every bid, email, and job log starts from a blank page.
- It takes you 20 minutes to write what should take 2.
- You know you're leaving money on the table in quoting, chasing, or collections — but you
  don't have the time to fix it.
- You've heard about AI, tried it once, and got something that "didn't sound like me."

### What this module is not
This is not a tech course. There's no programming, no data science, and no jargon you'll need
to look up. If you can send a text, you can use everything taught in this module.
