## How each lesson works

Every lesson follows the same rhythm, so you always know where you are:

1. **What you'll learn** — the skills, stated up front.
2. **Short, plain-English parts** — each part is always told through real concrete jobs.
3. **Copy-paste prompts** — exact text in a shaded box. Paste it into any free AI app, put in
   your numbers, and you're working.
4. **Try it yourself** — a 2–7 minute exercise using a real job from your own week.
5. **Checklist** — what "done" looks like, so you know you've actually got it.
6. **Quick reference card** — a one-page summary to print or keep on your phone.

**The promise:** every skill in this module is usable the same day. By the end of your first
lesson, you'll have sent at least one real email, draft, or post written with AI's help.
