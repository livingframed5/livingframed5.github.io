## What's inside

This module has 6 lessons, in the order we recommend working through them:

1. **Lesson 0 — Welcome.** A 10-minute start: what AI actually is, what it's good at (and bad
   at) for your business, and the one method every lesson uses.

2. **Lesson 1 — Everyday AI Tools: The Basics.** Set up your free AI app and use it for the
   stuff you do every day: bids, emails, site notes, marketing, and voice notes while you're
   driving between jobs.

3. **Lesson 2 — Estimation & Bidding with AI.** Rough-in quantities from real job facts, turn
   a scope paragraph into a clean line-item bid, and draft complete proposals — without
   starting from zero. AI builds the skeleton; you price, check, and own the number.

4. **Lesson 3 — Job Site & Operations AI.** Clean daily logs, draft pour and crew plans, turn
   job photos into QA and safety records, and run a 60-second morning check so weather,
   orders, and inspectors don't surprise you.

5. **Lesson 4 — Business & Finance AI.** Get invoices paid faster, categorize a month of
   expenses in minutes, run an honest cash-flow "runway" check, and finally explain your own
   numbers. Plus job ads and day-one sheets that attract good finishers.

6. **Lesson 5 — AI Guardrails, Privacy & Safety.** The short lesson that keeps you protected:
   what to never paste into an AI tool, how to double-check anything AI produces, and how to
   set simple rules for your crew.

> **Do I have to go in order?** No. Every lesson works on its own. If estimating is your pain
> point, start at Lesson 2. If getting paid is the problem, start at Lesson 4.
