## Part 4 — Monthly Review (the 20-minute habit)

Once a month, do this with your actual exports:

> "Here are my real numbers for [month]: income $[x], expenses by category [paste]. Compare to my
> target/typical month [$[x] income, $[x] materials, etc. if you have it]. In 5 bullet points,
> tell me: what changed, the biggest cost surprise, whether we hit target, and 2 actions to take
> next month. Flag anything that doesn't match my history as 'CHECK'."

Do it on the 1st of the month, and you'll know your business better than most owners — because
most owners never sit down with their own numbers organized.
