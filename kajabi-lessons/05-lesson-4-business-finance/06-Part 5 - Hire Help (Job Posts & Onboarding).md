## Part 5 — Hire Help (Job Posts & Onboarding)

AI writes the ad; you write the truth about the job.

> "You are a recruiter for a concrete contractor. Write a job ad for a concrete finisher:
> - Duties: form, place, finish, cure flatwork; stamp and exposed aggregate experience a plus.
> - Requirements: 3+ years finishing, own tools (trowels, floats), valid license/insurance,
>   reliable transport, must pass drug screen.
> - Pay: $[x]/hr DOE, weekly pay, benefits [list]. Job site location varies within [counties].
> Format: clear sections, no fluff, under 150 words, written so a good finisher wants to call."

**Onboarding FAQ for a new hire (same as the crew-assistant prompt in Lesson 3):**

> "Here are my company practices for new crew: [arrival, PPE, cell phones, breaks, who to call
> for what, truck/driver rules]. Create a one-page 'Day One' sheet a new hire can read in
> 5 minutes. Plain English, friendly but clear."
