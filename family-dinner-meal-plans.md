Two weeks of balanced dinners, shopping list, and beginner-friendly recipes.

# Recipes

## WEEK 1

### 1. Spaghetti with Meat Sauce
**Serves 5 | Prep 10 min | Cook 35 min**

Ingredients: 1 lb ground beef, 1 onion (diced), 3 cloves garlic (minced), 1 jar (24 oz) tomato sauce + 1 can diced tomatoes, 1 tsp each Italian seasoning & sugar, salt & pepper, 1 lb spaghetti, parmesan, olive oil.

1. Bring a large pot of **salted** water to a rolling boil.
2. Heat a drizzle of oil in a large skillet over **medium-high**. Add beef, break it apart with a spoon. Cook until no pink remains (~8 min). If there's a lot of grease, tilt pan and spoon it out.
3. Add onion, cook 3–4 min until soft. Add garlic, cook 1 min (don't let it burn).
4. Pour in sauce + diced tomatoes + seasoning + sugar (sugar cuts acidity). Stir. Reduce heat to **low**, cover, simmer **20 min**, stirring occasionally.
5. Meanwhile add spaghetti to boiling water. Cook per package (~8–10 min). **Taste a strand at 8 min**—you want it soft but slightly firm (al dente). Drain.
6. Mix sauce into pasta. Top with parmesan.

**Done when:** meat sauce is thick, no raw-meat smell, pasta offers slight resistance when bitten.

### 2. Sheet-Pan Chicken Fajitas
**Serves 5 | Prep 15 min | Cook 20 min**

Ingredients: 1.5 lbs chicken thighs/breast (sliced thin), 3 bell peppers (sliced), 1 onion (sliced), 2 tbsp oil, 1 packet fajita seasoning (or 1 tbsp chili powder + 1 tsp cumin + 1 tsp garlic powder + 1/2 tsp salt), 10 tortillas, toppings (sour cream, cheese, cilantro, lime).

1. Preheat oven to **425°F**. Line a baking sheet with foil.
2. In a bowl, toss chicken + peppers + onion with oil and seasoning until fully coated.
3. Spread in a **single layer** on the sheet (crowding = steaming, not browning).
4. Roast **20 min**, then check: chicken should be **165°F inside** (cut a thick piece to check—no pink). If not, roast 5 more min.
5. Warm tortillas in microwave 30 sec stacked between paper towels.
6. Build your own fajitas with toppings.

### 3. Slow-Cooker Beef Stew
**Serves 5–6 | Prep 20 min | Cook 8 hrs low**

Ingredients: 2 lbs beef chuck (cut into 1" cubes), 4 carrots (chunks), 1 lb potatoes (chunks), 2 stalks celery (slices), 1 onion (diced), 4 cups beef broth, 3 tbsp flour + extra to toss meat, 1 tbsp oil, 1 tsp thyme, salt & pepper.

1. Toss beef cubes in a bowl with flour + salt + pepper.
2. Heat oil in a skillet over **medium-high**. Brown beef in **batches** (don't crowd the pan), ~3 min per side until dark brown. This is flavor—don't skip.
3. Put beef in the slow cooker, then everything else on top (veg, broth, thyme). Stir once.
4. Cook **LOW 8 hours** or **HIGH 4–5 hours**. Do **not** lift the lid during cooking.
5. Thicken: mix 3 tbsp flour with 1/4 cup cool water, stir in, cook 20 more min on HIGH.

**Done when:** beef shreds apart with a fork, broth has thickened slightly.

### 4. Turkey Meatball Stir-Fry
**Serves 5 | Prep 10 min | Cook 15 min**

Ingredients: 1 bag frozen turkey meatballs (~24), 1 head broccoli (cut into florets), 2 bell peppers (sliced), 1/4 cup teriyaki or soy sauce, 2 tbsp honey, 2 tsp cornstarch + 2 tbsp water, 2 cups cooked rice, oil.

1. Start rice (or use instant rice packets).
2. Heat oil in a large skillet or wok over **high**. Add broccoli + peppers, stir-fry **3–4 min** until bright green and crisp-tender.
3. Push veg aside; add meatballs. Cook **5–7 min**, turning, until browned and **165°F inside** (if frozen, follow package time).
4. Whisk teriyaki + honey together; add to pan. In a small bowl mix cornstarch + water; stir that in too. Simmer **2 min** until sauce thickens and coats everything.
5. Serve over rice.

### 5. Homemade Pizza Night
**Serves 5 | Prep 20 min | Cook 15 min**

Ingredients: 2 pkg store-bought pizza dough, 1 cup pizza sauce, 3 cups shredded mozzarella, toppings (pepperoni, sausage, peppers, mushrooms), flour for dusting, olive oil.

1. Preheat oven to **450°F** (put a rack on the lowest position). Place a sheet pan or pizza stone inside to preheat too—this gives a crisp crust.
2. Let dough sit out 20 min to warm. On a floured surface, stretch it with your hands (push from center outward) into rounds. Don't obsess over shape.
3. Transfer to parchment paper. Brush edges lightly with olive oil.
4. Spread a thin layer of sauce (too much = soggy). Add cheese then toppings.
5. Slide onto the hot pan. Bake **12–15 min** until cheese is bubbly and edges are golden-brown.

**Done when:** crust bottom is browned and firm, cheese is melted in the center (not just edges).

### 6. Taco Bar
**Serves 5 | Prep 10 min | Cook 10 min**

Ingredients: 1 lb ground beef or turkey, 1 packet taco seasoning + 3/4 cup water, 10 hard or soft taco shells, toppings: shredded lettuce, diced tomatoes, cheese, sour cream, avocado, salsa.

1. Brown meat in a skillet over **medium-high**, breaking it up (~7 min until no pink). Drain excess grease.
2. Add seasoning + water, stir. Simmer **5 min** until most liquid is gone.
3. Set out shells + all toppings in separate bowls. Everyone builds their own.

### 7. Roast Chicken Dinner
**Serves 5 | Prep 10 min | Cook ~90 min**

Ingredients: 1 whole chicken (~4–5 lbs), 3 tbsp softened butter, 2 tbsp herbs (rosemary, thyme, sage or just herbs de Provence), salt & pepper, 3 lbs potatoes (quartered), 1 head broccoli, 3 tbsp olive oil.

1. Preheat oven to **425°F**.
2. Pat chicken dry with paper towels (this = crisp skin). Rub butter + herbs + salt on top all over. Place breast-side up in a roasting pan.
3. Toss potatoes with 2 tbsp oil + salt. Scatter around the chicken.
4. Roast for **~75–85 min** (about 20 min per pound). The real test: insert a meat thermometer into the thickest part of the thigh (not touching bone)—needs **165°F**. If you don't have a thermometer, cut between thigh and body: juices should run clear, not pink.
5. Remove chicken, rest **10–15 min** loosely covered with foil (this keeps it juicy) while you roast broccoli in oil at the same temp for 12 min.
6. Carve, serve. Leftover chicken = perfect sandwich meat.

## WEEK 2

### 8. Baked Ziti
**Serves 5–6 | Prep 15 min | Cook 30 min**

Ingredients: 1 lb ziti/pasta shells, 1 jar marinara + 1 can crushed tomatoes, 15 oz ricotta, 2 cups shredded mozzarella, 1 cup parmesan, 1 egg, 1/4 cup parsley, salt, oil.

1. Preheat **375°F**. Cook pasta 2 min *less* than package time (it finishes in the oven). Drain.
2. In a bowl, mix ricotta + egg + parmesan + parsley + salt.
3. Combine sauce + pasta. Fold in ricotta mixture (don't overmix—streaks are fine).
4. Pour into a 9x13 baking dish. Top with mozzarella.
5. Bake **25 min** until bubbling at the edges. Optional: broil **2 min** for browned spots.

### 9. Teriyaki Salmon (or Chicken)
**Serves 5 | Prep 5 min | Cook 15 min**

Ingredients: 1.5 lbs salmon fillets (or chicken thighs), 1/3 cup teriyaki sauce, 2 tbsp honey, 2 cups rice, 1 bag frozen edamame, oil, lemon.

1. Start rice. Boil edamame in salted water **5 min**, drain.
2. Preheat oven to **400°F**. Line a pan with foil and brush with oil.
3. Pat salmon dry. Mix teriyaki + honey, brush on top (save half for basting).
4. Bake **12–15 min**. Salmon is done when it **flakes** easily with a fork and is opaque in the center (check the thickest part). If using chicken, cook to **165°F** (~25 min).
5. Baste with remaining glaze, serve over rice with edamame and a squeeze of lemon.

### 10. Chili with Cornbread
**Serves 5–6 | Prep 10 min | Cook 40 min**

Ingredients: 1 lb ground beef, 1 onion (diced), 2 cans kidney beans (drained), 2 cans crushed tomatoes, 2 tbsp chili powder, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp garlic powder, salt, 1 box cornbread mix + ingredients it calls for.

1. Brown beef + onion in a pot over **medium-high** (~8 min). Drain grease.
2. Add everything else. Bring to a boil, then reduce to **low**, simmer **30 min**, stirring now and then. Taste and add salt—brands vary.
3. Make cornbread per box instructions in a separate dish *while chili simmers* (usually 400°F, 15–20 min; check with a toothpick—comes out clean when done).
4. Serve chili topped with cheese + sour cream, cornbread on the side.

### 11. Burrito Bowls
**Serves 5 | Prep 10 min | Cook 0 (assemble)**

Ingredients: 2 cups cooked rice, 2 cans black beans (rinsed), 1 can corn (drained), leftover taco meat or chili (or 1 lb cooked seasoned chicken), 2 avocados, 2 limes, cilantro, hot sauce.

1. Warm beans in a pot, warm corn 2 min, warm protein.
2. Build bowls: rice on bottom, then beans, corn, protein.
3. Top with avocado, cilantro, lime juice, hot sauce. Everyone assembles their own.

### 12. Chicken Burgers
**Serves 5 | Prep 10 min | Cook 15 min**

Ingredients: 1.5 lbs ground chicken, 1/2 cup breadcrumbs, 1 egg, 2 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp each pepper & paprika, 5 burger buns, toppings, 1 tbsp oil.

1. Mix chicken + breadcrumbs + egg + seasonings with your hands (lightly—overmixing = tough).
2. Form **5 equal patties**, about 1/2" thick. Make a slight thumbprint in the center so they cook flat.
3. Heat oil in a skillet over **medium** (chicken burns at high heat). Cook **6–7 min per side**.
4. **Crucial:** ground chicken must reach **165°F** inside—cut one open, no pink, or use a thermometer. 3–4 min before done, add cheese + a splash of water and cover to melt.
5. Toast buns in the same skillet, assemble.

### 13. Shrimp Pad Thai
**Serves 5 | Prep 15 min | Cook 10 min**

Ingredients: 12 oz rice noodles, 1 lb shrimp (peeled) or cubed tofu, 2 eggs, 1/3 cup soy sauce, 3 tbsp lime juice, 2 tbsp sugar (or brown), 1 tbsp peanut butter, 3 cloves garlic (minced), 1/2 cup scallions + cilantro, 1/4 cup peanuts (chopped), oil, bean sprouts (optional).

1. Soak noodles in hot water per package (~10 min), until pliable but firm. Drain well.
2. Whisk sauce: soy + lime + sugar + peanut butter + 1/4 cup water.
3. Heat oil in a wok over **high**. Add garlic 30 sec, then shrimp, cook **2–3 min** until pink and curled (don't overcook).
4. Push shrimp aside, add beaten eggs, scramble 1 min.
5. Add noodles + sauce. Toss vigorously **2–3 min** so sauce coats everything.
6. Off heat: stir in scallions, cilantro, peanuts. Serve with extra lime.

### 14. Slow-Cooker Pot Roast
**Serves 5–6 | Prep 20 min | Cook 8 hrs low**

Ingredients: 3 lbs beef chuck roast, 4 carrots (chunks), 1 lb potatoes (chunks), 1 lb green beans (trimmed), 1 onion (quartered), 3 cups beef broth, 3 tbsp Worcestershire, 2 tbsp flour, salt & pepper, oil.

1. Pat roast dry, rub with salt + pepper + flour.
2. Heat oil in a skillet over **high**. Sear roast **3–4 min per side** until dark crust forms.
3. Place roast in slow cooker, arrange veg around it, pour broth + Worcestershire over.
4. Cook **LOW 8 hours**. Add green beans in the **last 30–40 min** so they stay green and crisp.
5. **Done when:** meat pulls apart with a fork (fall-apart tender). Rest 10 min, slice against the grain.
