Welcome to the Nexus Cloud Partner Program. This guide walks new partners through getting productive in the partner portal for the first time.

To log in, go to portal.nexus.cloud and click "Sign in". Use the work email registered in your partner agreement. The first sign-in creates your account from the invitation email you received from your onboarding manager.

After sign-in, complete your company profile: legal entity name, billing address, and primary contact. This profile is required before you can place orders or draw down co-marketing funds.

Your portal home page shows your assigned tier, your account manager contact, active agreements, and a snapshot of your current annual contracted value (ACV) versus committed minimums.

Bookmark the Help Center, which is linked from the top navigation bar. It contains the full policy library, a searchable knowledge base, and the support ticket form.

If you cannot sign in after three attempts, open a ticket from the portal login page so the support team can check your invitation status. Do not create a second account while a ticket is open.
