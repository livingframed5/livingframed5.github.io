How to use ContractScope

ContractScope turns an MSA / SOW / RFP excerpt into a scoping brief for pursuit & account teams: extracted variables, risk flags with suggested clauses, compliance checks, AI-style recommendation runs, and an audit-ready export.

Everything runs in your browser — no data leaves your machine.

STEP 1

Paste (or load a sample)

Go to Ingest and paste your MSA, SOW, or RFP text into the box. Not sure what to try? Click Sample: dMPS MSA or Sample: State Gov SOW for a ready-made demo. Add the pursuit name / region / ACV and any custom watchwords you want flagged.

STEP 2

Run the pipeline

Click “Run Scoping Pipeline” — everything pastes into the tabs. The chip in the top-right changes from “No analysis yet” to your scope ID, instrument, and score.

STEP 3

Read what was parsed

Scoping Parser lists every contract variable the engine found (term, auto-renew, SLA credits, liability cap, labor/CPI, billing basis, FAR/DFARS/CLIN, GDPR⋯) with a confidence % and the clause it came from.

STEP 4

Review risks

Risk Assessment shows the compliance score and every High / Moderate / Low finding. Each card has the evidence, a recommendation, and a ready-to-paste suggested clause your team can take into the negotiation.

STEP 5

Ask the prompt library

Prompt Library holds 9 pre-built workflows (gov cross-check, SLA quantifier, revenue-recognition, labor-rate, term/exit, data-security, custom red-flags, audit report). Press Run sandbox on any card to see an evidence-grounded answer; Copy spec grabs the filled system+user prompt.

STEP 6

Check compliance & export

Compliance X-Ref flags governance controls to validate (FAR, DFARS, CLIN, booking, revenue, labor, data, cyber). Audit & Export previews the report and downloads it as Markdown, HTML, CSV, or JSON — or prints it.

My text pasted — but “No analysis yet” is still showing? You haven’t clicked Run Scoping Pipeline yet — pasting alone doesn’t trigger the analysis. Click the button (or a sample) and the chip will update.
Something flagged incorrectly? Extractions are heuristic. Open data/patterns.js to tune patterns, data/rules.js to add/edit risk rules, and data/prompts.js for prompts. Full architecture: docs/ARCHITECTURE.md.
Want real LLM answers? The “Run sandbox” button uses the deterministic engine. The single seam to plug in your own model is ContractScope.query.runPrompt in js/report.js.
