Deal model & analysis

Financial engine · cost allocation · guardrail-aware

Revenue build-up (monthly · yr 1)

Click revenue = mono pages × (list × (1−tier disc.)) + colour pages × (list × (1−tier disc.)). Hardware = lease cost × (1+markup) × (1−discount), billed onward. Labour = billable hours × fully-burdened hourly rate × (1+markup). Add per-device hardware/software service fees and the one-time setup fee.

Fully-burdened labour

Rate includes salary, payroll taxes, benefits, tooling and overhead loading. Utilization turns scheduled hours into billable hours; hours not billed stay a cost — this is the mechanism behind the Sensitivity pane.

TCV / ACV / margin

TCV = Σ over term of (monthly revenue × 12 × volume growth) + setup fee. ACV = TCV ÷ term. Gross margin = (revenue − direct cost) ÷ revenue. Net profit = gross − corporate overhead allocation − one-time deployment + setup fee, all over the contract term.

Business Controls

Five gates: gross margin, net margin, labour markup, blended-discount ceiling (volume + hardware concessions) and SLA exposure. Each scores PASS / WARN / FAIL against corporate floors you can edit on the Compliance tab — FAILs flag the deal for review before booking.
