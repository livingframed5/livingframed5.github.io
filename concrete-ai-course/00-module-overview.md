# Welcome to the AI for Concrete Contractors Module

**Time to complete this module:** about 3 hours across 6 lessons. Each lesson is short (under
10 minutes), so you can knock one out between pours, in the office, or on your phone.

---

## This module is for you if...

You run a concrete business — flatwork, driveways, foundations, stamped, exposed aggregate,
or ready-mix — and the office side of things is eating your day:

- Every bid, email, and job log starts from a blank page.
- It takes you 20 minutes to write what should take 2.
- You know you're leaving money on the table in quoting, chasing, or collections — but you
  don't have the time to fix it.
- You've heard about AI, tried it once, and got something that "didn't sound like me."

### What this module is not
This is not a tech course. There's no programming, no data science, and no jargon you'll need
to look up. If you can send a text, you can use everything taught in this module.

---

## What you'll be able to do when you're done

| Right now | After this module |
|---|---|
| Blank-page dread before every bid | Draft a professional bid and proposal in minutes, then make it yours |
| Emails that stack up for hours | Summarize long client emails and specs in seconds and answer faster |
| Re-typing the same site notes every day | Turn a messy voice note into a clean daily job log |

## What's inside

This module has 6 lessons, in the order we recommend working through them:

1. **Lesson 0 — Welcome.** A 10-minute start: what AI actually is, what it's good at (and bad
   at) for your business, and the one method every lesson uses.

2. **Lesson 1 — Everyday AI Tools: The Basics.** Set up your free AI app and use it for the
   stuff you do every day: bids, emails, site notes, marketing, and voice notes while you're
   driving between jobs.

3. **Lesson 2 — Estimation & Bidding with AI.** Rough-in quantities from real job facts, turn
   a scope paragraph into a clean line-item bid, and draft complete proposals — without
   starting from zero. AI builds the skeleton; you price, check, and own the number.

4. **Lesson 3 — Job Site & Operations AI.** Clean daily logs, draft pour and crew plans, turn
   job photos into QA and safety records, and run a 60-second morning check so weather,
   orders, and inspectors don't surprise you.

5. **Lesson 4 — Business & Finance AI.** Get invoices paid faster, categorize a month of
   expenses in minutes, run an honest cash-flow "runway" check, and finally explain your own
   numbers. Plus job ads and day-one sheets that attract good finishers.

6. **Lesson 5 — AI Guardrails, Privacy & Safety.** The short lesson that keeps you protected:
   what to never paste into an AI tool, how to double-check anything AI produces, and how to
   set simple rules for your crew.

> **Do I have to go in order?** No. Every lesson works on its own. If estimating is your pain
> point, start at Lesson 2. If getting paid is the problem, start at Lesson 4.

## How each lesson works

Every lesson follows the same rhythm, so you always know where you are:

1. **What you'll learn** — the skills, stated up front.
2. **Short, plain-English parts** — each part is always told through real concrete jobs.
3. **Copy-paste prompts** — exact text in a shaded box. Paste it into any free AI app, put in
   your numbers, and you're working.
4. **Try it yourself** — a 2–7 minute exercise using a real job from your own week.
5. **Checklist** — what "done" looks like, so you know you've actually got it.
6. **Quick reference card** — a one-page summary to print or keep on your phone.

**The promise:** every skill in this module is usable the same day. By the end of your first
lesson, you'll have sent at least one real email, draft, or post written with AI's help.

---

## A few honest notes before you start

- **AI drafts, you decide.** The tools in this module are great at speed, structure, and first
  drafts. They're not great at knowing your local prices, your crew, or your site. Every lesson
  shows you exactly where to check the work.
- **It's a method, not a magic button.** AI won't run your jobs. It'll take the busywork off
  your plate so you can focus on the work that pays.
- **Your data stays yours.** Lesson 5 covers what to keep out of AI tools and how to protect
  your clients, your crew, and your pricing.

You know how to pour. Let's make the office side as smooth as your best finish.
