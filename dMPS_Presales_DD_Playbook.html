Purpose, Scope & How to Use

One operating playbook that connects opportunity consulting, risk assurance, and due-diligence design coordination from first qualification through final deal sign-off — and leaves an auditable trail.

What this playbook governs

Complex enterprise managed print & device services (dMPS) pursuits — multi-site fleets, custom pricing, service-level commitments, financing structures, third-party labor, and multi-entity contracts.

Who runs it

The Deal Assurance Control Room: pursuit lead, finance controller (deal economics), business controls/compliance, legal counsel, and delivery design — operating on one checklist and one risk register.

What it produces

(1) a phased DD roadmap, (2) RACI-protected accountability, (3) a scored risk & escalation framework, (4) an audit-ready business controls checklist, and (5) a documented artifact & sign-off repository.

How evidence is proven

Every checkpoint has an owner + evidence. Every risk has a mitigation owner and a sign-off band. Nothing reaches final sign-off with open Critical/High or unapproved red-flag items.

How to use this tool
  1. Setup (5 min): In the Deal Context tab fill in opportunity metadata, target dates, and confirm the pursuit roster. The header chip updates automatically.
  2. Drive the phases: Work top-to-bottom through the workflow tab. Each phase lists activities, gates, and artifacts with its RACI anchor.
  3. Score risk early: Register every concern in the risk engine. Likelihood × Impact auto-bands the risk and dictates escalation path & mandatory sign-off.
  4. Validate on the checklist: Complete the business controls as work is validated. Each block shows live % complete; the Pre-Submit bar gates the submission.
  5. Track artifacts & sign-offs: Use the artifact register to trace each deliverable to an owner, status, evidence, and approver. This becomes your audit-proof pack.
  6. Close the loop: The governance tab defines cadence, standing agenda, and the metrics you report to the approval board. State persists in this browser; export JSON before archiving.
Operating principles
  • No price exception without risk banding: every non-standard pricing & SLA clause is mirrored by a risk line with a named mitigation owner.
  • One version of the truth: risk register, checklist, and artifact tracker always reflect the same deal snapshot. No parallel logs.
  • Sign-off follows the money: booking, recognition, and margin gates are controlled by Finance, independent of the pursuit team.
  • Audit-ready at every cutoff: the pack must survive an internal audit or governance review on any working day, not just at submission.

End-to-End Workflow, Timeline & Gates

Phased roadmap from qualification to final sign-off, with a disposition gate at each milestone. Cadence is indicative for a medium-complexity deal (~60–80 business days); compress or extend per the complexity band set in Section 2.

Gate discipline

Every phase ends in a Go / Conditional-Go / No-Go (G0–G6). No phase is entered before the gate above is closed by the accountable owner. A No-Go parks the deal, not the checklist.

Complexity banding

L/M: standard factory — compressed calendar. H: full playbook required. XXL: global fleets / multi-entity / financing — extended calendar with CFO & Compliance attendance at every gate.

  1. P0Qualification & Intake· Days 0–5G0 · Qualify/Kill
    • Opportunity registered in CRM with booking code, owner, and forecast class.
    • Red-flag screen applied (sanctions, credit, conflict of interest, export/regulatory). Kill criteria checked before resource burn.
    • Business case outline: strategy fit, rough margin, capacity, competitive must-win view.
    • Pursuit roster assigned to complexity band; NDA confirmed before any baseline data access.
  2. P1Discovery, Baseline & Data Capture· Days 5–20G1 · Baseline
    • Customer device inventory, print volumes, monthly pages, and service history collected via a structured Intake Workbook.
    • Site survey plan scoped (sites, sampling, remote vs on-site); executed with controlled records and photo/evidence logs.
    • Third-party dependencies inventoried (break/fix subcon, OEM warranty, logistics). Baseline sign-off by Deal Lead; discrepancies become risk lines.
    • Pricing data set is version-stamped — a single source of truth workbook.
  3. P2Solution Design, Cost Build & Pricing· Days 15–35G2 · Cost
    • Service-design blueprint: fleet refresh, people model, labor pools, break-fix, stock strategy, tooling.
    • Cost build in the corporate cost workbook: component-level TCO (fleet/lease, labor with utilization, toner/parts, transition, software).
    • Pricing model produced (CPP tiers, labor, transition, invoicing structure). All non-standard pricing mirrored to the risk register.
    • Costing sign-off by Pricing Analyst + Finance Controller at the cost gate.
  4. P3Risk Quantification, Mitigation & Escalation· Days 25–42G3 · Risk
    • Full risk register scored (likelihood × impact), mitigated, and signed under the escalation bands.
    • Red-flag items swept at every node — none hidden. Contingencies quantified (baseline + transition + SLA).
    • Risk register signed by Finance Controls at the Risk Gate before the offer is finalized.
  5. P4Contract, Legal, Security & Terms Review· Days 30–46G4 · Legal
    • Draft contract / schedules reviewed: SLAs, termination, liability caps, indexation, insurance, taxes, DPA/SCCs.
    • Non-standard terms logged in the Terms Exception Log and mirrored into risk / contingency.
    • Infosec cleared for data and endpoint scope; any PII / third-party flows sanctioned.
  6. P5Business Controls, Booking & Pre-Submit Validation· Days 44–52G5 · Controls
    • Business controls checklist completed to 100% by outcome — no blockers.
    • Order-booking, cost-management, labor-allocation, and revenue-recognition reviews closed by Finance Controller.
    • Artifact pack assembled and every artifact signed. Pre-submit attestation by Business Controls / Compliance.
  7. P6Final Approval & Deal Sign-Off· Days 52–60G6 · DOA
    • Approval for the DOA band obtained (sponsor chain / CFO for H and XXL).
    • Final GO: all gates green OR approved TIBA waivers filed on the official record.
    • Deal milestone locked in system (forecast + booking intent); execution-hands on planned.
  8. P7Handover, Transition & Embedding Controls· Days 60–75 (post-sign)Handover
    • Signed contract, baselines, and risk register handed to Delivery / Transition with named owners.
    • Contract Manager creates the clauses matrix; project code & cost structure set; onboarding plan activated.
    • Post-sign compliance check at t-30/60/90 confirms cost vs plan and no booking leaks.

Score every risk on Likelihood × Impact (1–5). The engine auto-computes the band, the mandatory sign-off chain, and the mitigation protocol. Nothing is submitted without its risk line closed.

Score bands & mandatory response
Low (1–4) — Standard offer with a named risk owner; tracked on the register only. No escalation required.
Medium (5–9) — Requires a mitigation plan plus Finance Controller and Deal Lead sign-off before submission.
High (10–15) — Requires a formal mitigation plan, Business Controls + Legal review, and written sign-off. No GO without an approved waiver (TIBA). Escalates via Section 5 path.
Critical (16–25) — Deal does not sign without Executive Sponsor / CFO sign-off, Compliance endorsement, and an approved TIBA or exemption. Board-level review required.
Red-flag / auto-escalation triggers

• Any deal that would run with negative or below-threshold margin at forecast volumes. • Sanctions, KYC/AML, export, or regulatory red flags at screening. • Unlimited liability or an indemnity cap below the corporate floor (or any clause the company cannot bind). • Custom SLA: penalty exposure with no agreed measurement / baseline for the metric. • Guaranteed savings clauses without an enforceable delivery process owning them. • Customer credit score, adverse financials, or dispute history that fails credit approval. • New country / entity launch not enabled for this line of business (tax, employment, data residency). • A deviation from the DOA, or non-standard terms without a waiver on file. • Any requested change to order-booking, recognition, or revenue rules.

Rule: a red-flag trigger cannot be cleared below Executive Sponsor + Compliance sign-off and must be logged in the Escalation Log with the decision and approved record.

# High/Critical open, # mitigated, # waivers, red-flag count — trended week over week.

Velocity & quality

Days per phase vs plan; % of gates passed at first attempt; count of deals re-spun at G5 — an early-warning signal.

Audit trail rules (for internal audit / governance review)

1. Immutable log: the Escalation & Decision Log (Section 5) and sign-off dates (Section 7) are the formal record. Appends only; no deletions without Business Controls approval. 2. Version stamping: artifacts follow the stamping rule in Section 7; version history retained in the repository. 3. Who-what-when: each control in Section 6 is tied to a named verifier. Attestation means the named people attest. 4. Exemptions: any deliberate override of a control is recorded as a TIBA / waiver artifact routed via the Section 5 escalation path — never silent. 5. Archival: at deal end, export this playbook JSON and store it in the contract folder of the repository. Retention per the record schedule.

SLAService level agreement — committed metric + remedy. Measurement must exist or the SLA is a red flag.
UtilizationBillable vs paid hours for labor; non-billable, standby, and warranty hours quantified and priced.
RecognitionRevenue recognition policy mapping contract obligations to a recognising period (e.g., over time vs point in time).
BookingOrder-booking rule set — what value can be booked at signing and what is deferred.
